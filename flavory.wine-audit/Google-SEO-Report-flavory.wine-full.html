1. Executive Summary

This report presents a comprehensive SEO audit of flavory.wine for a D2C e-commerce (WooCommerce): wine tasting game boxes, B2B gifts, on-location tastings; nl-NL primary with partial /de/ section site, generated on September 16, 2026. Findings combine technical, content, schema, performance, visual, and search-readiness evidence as available.

  1. Info: Server response time of 3 to 7 seconds on every page; lab LCP 5.0 s on the mobile homepage. Cache-Control max-age=0 plus WooCommerce session and __cvg_1p_uid cookies on anonymous requests defeat page caching.
  2. Info: No Product, Offer, AggregateRating or Review schema on any product page, so Merchant listings and star ratings are unreachable despite a 4.5-star Trustpilot rating shown on page.
  3. Info: Every unknown URL 301-redirects to the homepage; 26 legacy product URLs linked 287 times from 114 pages and the privacy policy URL all land on the homepage.

  4. Trash or 301 the test pages, German clones and kerstcadeau variants; noindex ?reviews=/?store= and geen-categorie; disable the author sitemap.
  5. Add H1s and meta descriptions to /shop/, the product pages, /flavory-gameplay/, /wijnkiezer/, the B2B page and /proeverij/; add 'wijnbox' and 'wijnproeverij thuis' to titles.

2. Audit Categories

2.1 Technical SEO

Score: 34/100

What Works

  • Server-rendered HTML, no JavaScript rendering dependency
  • www and http correctly 301 to https://flavory.wine
  • Valid robots.txt and valid sitemap index with 8 child sitemaps
  • Viewport meta present on all pages; cart pages noindexed

Findings

Catch-all 301 to homepage instead of 404 (soft-404 pattern) Critical

Any unknown path returns 301 to https://flavory.wine/. 26 legacy product/landing URLs still linked 287 times from 114 pages land on the homepage; /privacybeleid/ is swallowed too.

Recommendation: Serve real 404s with a helpful template; retarget each legacy URL to its live product (map in findings/ecommerce.md); update internal links.

No hreflang and broken language roots Critical

0 of 208 pages carry hreflang. /de/, /en/, /nl 301 to the Dutch homepage; the German home is /de/start/. 60 of 96 German URLs resolve to Dutch pages via redirect or canonical. /en/shop/ and /en/blog/ declare en-US but serve Dutch.

Recommendation: Point /de/ to /de/start/; add hreflang nl/de/x-default only for translated pairs; noindex or remove the /en/ pages unless an English site is planned.

Index bloat: about 40% of crawled URLs should not be indexable High

9 builder test pages duplicating the homepage, 14 German -kopie/-copy product clones (99.4% identical), 7 kerstcadeau landing variants, duplicated blog posts, 22 thin ?reviews=/?store= pages, indexable geen-categorie archive. 46 of 120 sitemap URLs affected.

Recommendation: Trash or 301 duplicates, noindex CPT parameter pages and uncategorised archives, exclude them from the sitemap.

No security headers High

HSTS, CSP, X-Frame-Options, X-Content-Type-Options and Referrer-Policy are all absent.

Recommendation: Add the headers in the nginx server block; start CSP in report-only mode.

Page caching defeated by cookies and headers High

Cache-Control: max-age=0, Expires now, wp_woocommerce_session and __cvg_1p_uid cookies set on first anonymous request. TTFB 3.4 to 3.9 s with curl.

Recommendation: Full-page cache with cookie exclusions limited to cart/checkout/account; set proper cache headers.

Heavy HTML and flat internal linking Medium

Average HTML 314 KB (max 503 KB), 27 to 35 stylesheets and 28 to 32 blocking scripts per page; every blog post links to every other post and to the cart page; 14 sitemap URLs have no inbound links.

Recommendation: Remove unused builder CSS/JS; replace the all-posts related block with curated cluster links.

Findings

Recommendation: Page cache with cookie exclusions, correct headers, OPcache, object cache, CDN.

Recommendation: Remove unused builder assets, defer non-critical JS, inline critical CSS.

Recommendation: Delay non-essential tags until interaction; consolidate through GTM.

Heavy page weight and inconsistent lazy loading Medium

Homepage and blog transfer 9.4 to 9.7 MB cold; /shop/ lazy-loads 0 of 20 images.

Recommendation: Lazy-load below-fold images, compress media, audit videos.

INP not measured Info

No synthetic interaction fired; TBT used as proxy.

Recommendation: Re-measure with field data once GSC/CrUX is connected.
